Beer Counter

Read me

Beer Counter helps you log what you drink and compares it to a simple pace limit based on pure alcohol (ethanol) and a reference period you choose. Everything runs in the browser; your data stays on this device unless you clear site storage.

How to use

  • Quick presets. Tap a cell in the strength × size grid (2,0% / 3,5% / 5,0% and 20–50 cl) to log a drink with that ABV and volume.
  • Summary. Drank / allowed shows pure alcohol and approximate standard beers. The message line shows when the next standard beer is available, too late before going home, or past go-home time.
  • Log. Newest entries appear first. Tap a line to edit date, time, or preset. Undo last removes the newest entry.
  • Reference period. Use the dropdown (30–180 minutes) to set the pace. After the starter allowance, this is the wait between beers.
  • Go home time. Optional. When set, the app blocks new beers during the final reference period before leaving, then switches to a go-home message after that time.
  • Reset. Clears the current drink log. Reference period and go-home time stay set.

Logic and rules

  • Standard beer. One standard beer is 40 cl at 5%, which equals 2 cl pure alcohol.
  • Pure alcohol. Each drink is converted with volume × ABV. Example: 50 cl at 5% is 2,5 cl pure alcohol.
  • Starter allowance. The first 4 cl of pure alcohol, equal to two standard beers, can be started without waiting.
  • Cooldown. After the starter allowance, a new beer is OK only when one full reference period has passed since the latest real drink.
  • Go-home cutoff. Last normal beer start is go-home time minus the reference period. Before that, the app can show Next beer in X min. During the final window it shows No more beers before going home. After go-home time it shows No more beers go home.
  • Summary colours. Green means current drank is at or under allowed. Red means current drank is over allowed. There is no yellow status.
  • Preset colours. Green means starting that beer is OK now. Red means not OK because of cooldown or go-home cutoff.
  • Midnight sessions. A go-home time earlier than the first drink, such as 02:00 after starting at 16:00, is treated as the next day.

Features and notes

  • Persistence. Log and settings are stored locally in this browser. They survive closing the tab, but not another device or cleared site data.
  • No automatic daily reset. The drink log stays until you tap Reset. The session date is anchored to the first real drink, so after-midnight sessions keep working.
  • Not medical advice. The limits are a personal planning tool, not a safety or health guideline.
